# Release And QC

## Editorial QC

- Verify chronology and distinguish fact, feeling, and inference.
- Verify the father is not reduced to a single villain.
- Verify Duoduo is an involved participant, not a solved-problem mentor.
- Verify each visual adds information, emotion, evidence, or time/place context.
- Verify no B-roll shot repeats and no generic or fabricated history appears.
- Verify subtitles exactly match actual speech and do not split words or phrases.

## Technical QC

- Decode the full export and confirm zero offline media.
- Check dimensions, frame rate, codecs, duration, and audio channels.
- Listen for clipped edits, missing syllables, music masking, and excessive click cues.
- Target an intelligible social mix near -14 to -16 LUFS and no peak above -1 dBTP when the toolchain supports it.
- Check the first 5 seconds muted and the whole story audio-only.
- Inspect representative frames for skin, subtitle fit, black rounded corners, keyword typography, privacy, and platform safe zones.

## Cover

- Default Xiaohongshu cover: 3:4, 1080x1440, unless Duoduo approves another format.
- Use a serious real close-up, one relevant real object, and one conflict sentence.
- Use Source Han Serif SC Heavy or the approved editorial serif treatment.
- Use teal only as a small underline or accent, not as a large fill.
- Do not finalize the release package before Duoduo approves the object image and cover.

## Release Folder

After approval, place these together in the requested `To publish` folder:

- final video;
- approved cover PNG or JPG;
- final title;
- publishing copy;
- platform-specific title/copy variants when requested;
- a short facts/privacy note when the story includes sensitive evidence.

Publishing, uploading, scheduling, or external account actions still require explicit authorization.
