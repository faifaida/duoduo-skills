# SKW Video Editorial

Turn a business outcome into a narrative and then into an executable edit. Treat images, text, music, sound, and silence as narrative decisions rather than decoration.

## Workflow

1. Identify the video family in [video-types.md](references/video-types.md).
2. Define the audience's initial state, intended final state, and observable business outcome.
3. Select a story spine and write a beat map using [storytelling.md](references/storytelling.md).
4. Convert each beat into shot duties with [decupagem.md](references/decupagem.md).
5. Design energy, pace, interruption, and lettering with [rhythm-lettering.md](references/rhythm-lettering.md).
6. Build the audio argument using [sound-design.md](references/sound-design.md).
7. Translate the plan into Palmier Pro operations with [palmier-pro.md](references/palmier-pro.md).
8. Validate narrative, continuity, diversity, and delivery with [quality-control.md](references/quality-control.md).

Use [brief-template.md](assets/brief-template.md) before media selection. Use [shot-plan-template.md](assets/shot-plan-template.md) for decupagem and [edit-map-template.md](assets/edit-map-template.md) before timeline assembly.

## Editorial invariants

- Start from the result the video must enable, not from the available footage.
- Give every shot at least one duty: orient, reveal, prove, contrast, intensify, humanize, or transition.
- Remove a beautiful shot if it repeats information without increasing emotion, meaning, or rhythm.
- Build contrast across scale, subject, client, location, motion direction, color, and sound.
- Use lettering to challenge or reframe the image; do not merely caption it.
- Let audio lead selected cuts. Include at least one designed interruption when the concept benefits from surprise.
- Prefer raw footage without client watermarks, embedded graphics, or baked-in branding.
- Preserve enough screen time for comprehension. Fast does not mean uniformly short.
- Report assumptions and unavailable proof. Never invent business results.

## Output contract

Produce, in order:

1. one-sentence strategic premise;
2. audience transformation and business outcome;
3. story spine;
4. timed beat map;
5. shot-by-shot decupagem;
6. audio and lettering map;
7. footage search and rejection criteria;
8. Palmier Pro assembly plan;
9. QC checklist and unresolved evidence.

Keep source time in seconds and timeline placement in frames when using Palmier tools. Inspect media before describing or selecting it.
