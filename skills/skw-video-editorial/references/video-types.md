# Video families

Choose the closest family, then adapt it to the real objective.

| Family | Primary outcome | Narrative engine | Useful evidence | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demo reel / site hero | Create desire and qualify the producer | Range → disruption → point of view → consequence | Client diversity, craft range, human response | Pretty montage with no proposition |
| Case film | Prove strategic and execution value | Need → tension → audiovisual idea → execution → change → proof | Result, testimony, artifact, audience behavior | Retrospective chronology without conflict |
| Event aftermovie | Extend experience and trigger participation | Anticipation → arrival → escalation → collective peak → residue | Scale, reactions, rituals, sensory detail | Random highlights over continuous music |
| Employer branding | Strengthen belonging and culture | Individual truth → shared behavior → collective identity | Employees acting, collaborating, celebrating | Executives describing culture abstractly |
| Institutional / image bank | Build a reusable and credible brand world | System → people → process → impact | Coverage breadth, consistent visual grammar | Generic beauty shots with no usage map |
| Sales incentive | Move an audience toward action | Friction → opportunity → reward → action | Product benefit, seller recognition, CTA | Corporate explanation before desire |
| Performance bulletin | Make numbers understandable and trusted | Question → signal → interpretation → implication | Source, comparison, trend, spokesperson | Data dump without hierarchy |
| Health / wellbeing content | Turn knowledge into attainable behavior | Human situation → insight → practical possibility | Expert credibility, food/body/life detail | Moralizing or promising unsupported outcomes |

## Hybrid projects

Name one primary family and at most one secondary family. The primary family owns the opening, climax, and CTA. The secondary family may influence tone or evidence but must not create a second competing ending.
