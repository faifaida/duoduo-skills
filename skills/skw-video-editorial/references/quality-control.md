# Quality control

## Strategy

- The audience's initial and final states are explicit.
- The behavioral and business outcomes are plausible and distinguishable.
- The CTA follows from the story rather than appearing as a tag.
- Every claimed result has evidence or is clearly labeled as an objective.

## Story

- Every beat changes information, emotion, expectation, or direction.
- The dominant story engine remains legible.
- The opening creates a question the film later answers.
- The climax contains the strongest consequence, not merely the fastest montage.
- The designed surprise has setup and narrative payoff.

## Picture

- Every retained shot has a stated duty.
- Motion, focus, exposure, and framing are intentional.
- Adjacent shots vary usefully in scale, subject, movement, or meaning.
- Client and project repetition is controlled.
- No watermark or baked-in client graphic enters unintentionally.
- Continuity breaks are motivated.

## Audio and text

- Music phrases and cuts cooperate without becoming mechanical.
- Production sound establishes credibility and place.
- SFX are selective and materially appropriate.
- Silence remains audible as an editorial choice.
- Lettering reframes rather than duplicates the picture.
- Reading time matches the actual format and viewport.

## Delivery

- Offline media, unsupported formats, and missing audio are resolved.
- Safe areas and typography work in the target aspect ratio.
- Spoken content, claims, names, and figures are verified.
- Music, footage, fonts, and third-party marks have appropriate rights.
- Review export is checked from start to finish with sound.
